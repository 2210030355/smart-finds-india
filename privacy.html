Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy describes the Smart Finds India informational website and the planned Smart Finds Automation application. The application is currently being developed for management of our own Pinterest business account. Features described as planned are not yet active.

Information we process

This static website does not include a registration form or collect information directly through an account system. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may process technical request information under its own policies. When Pinterest API integration is enabled, our application is expected to access only authorized account, board, Pin and related operational information required for content management.

Purpose and legal use

Authorized Pinterest information will be used to prepare, organize, publish and review content for Smart Finds India's own account, subject to the permissions granted. We will not sell Pinterest account data or use it for unrelated advertising profiles.

Storage and security

The planned backend uses a private PostgreSQL database for product records, publishing status and operational metadata. API credentials and tokens will be kept outside public source code. Access will be restricted to authorized operators. We will review and delete information no longer needed for the stated purpose.

Third-party services

Our project may interact with Pinterest, Telegram, GitHub Pages and authorized affiliate providers. Their respective privacy policies govern information handled directly by those services. External links may take visitors to third-party sites.

Affiliate links

Some content may eventually include disclosed affiliate links. Qualifying purchases may earn a commission at no additional cost to the purchaser. We do not operate checkout or directly collect payment-card details through this website.

Your choices and requests

Users can revoke Pinterest permissions through their Pinterest account settings where supported. For access or deletion requests related to this application, contact the operator using the public contact channel linked from the project's GitHub repository once published. This page must be updated with a working contact method before submitting the app for review.

Changes

We may update this policy as the application evolves, especially before enabling new data collection or public access. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Before submitting to Pinterest: add a real, working contact method and verify every statement against the actual application configuration.
